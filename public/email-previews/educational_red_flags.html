Hello Alex,

After a scam, victims are often targeted again by fake “recovery” services. Protect yourself with these warning signs:

• Upfront crypto payment — legitimate forensic firms do not demand BTC/USDT before any work.

• Guaranteed 100% recovery — no ethical analyst can promise full recovery; outcomes depend on fund location.

• Pressure to share seed phrases — never provide private keys or seed words by email or chat.

• No verifiable business address — check domain age, reviews, and corporate registration.

• Anonymous Telegram-only support — insist on official domain email (@cryptorecoveryasset.com).

Crypto Recovery Asset publishes transparent procedures, verified reviews, and secure client portals. If you are comparing providers, we welcome due diligence.
